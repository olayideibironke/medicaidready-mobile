# Privacy Policy for MedicaidReady

Last Updated: June 2026

MedicaidReady ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the MedicaidReady mobile application.

## Information We Collect

MedicaidReady is designed to provide educational information and preliminary Medicaid eligibility screening.

The application may collect information that you voluntarily enter, including:

* State of residence
* Household size
* Income information
* Pregnancy status
* Disability status
* Other eligibility-related responses

## How We Use Information

Information entered into the application is used solely to:

* Estimate potential Medicaid eligibility
* Display eligibility guidance
* Improve application functionality

## Data Storage

MedicaidReady does not sell personal information.

Information entered during eligibility screening is not shared with government agencies and does not constitute an official Medicaid application.

Some information may be temporarily processed to provide eligibility results.

## Analytics

The application may collect anonymous usage information to improve performance, reliability, and user experience.

This information may include:

* Device type
* Operating system version
* Application performance metrics
* Crash reports

## Third-Party Services

MedicaidReady may utilize third-party services including:

* Apple App Store
* Google Play Store
* Expo
* Analytics and crash reporting providers

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

MedicaidReady is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

## Medical and Legal Disclaimer

MedicaidReady provides educational information only.

The application does not provide medical advice, legal advice, or official eligibility determinations.

Only the appropriate state Medicaid agency can determine official eligibility and approve benefits.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information and maintain application security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted within the application and on our public repository.
